## Object of the Game

The first person to collect the required number of Unicorns in their **Stable** wins!

The number of Unicorns you need to collect depends on the number of players:

* **2 Players:** 7 Unicorns
* **3-5 Players:** 7 Unicorns
* **6-8 Players:** 6 Unicorns

## Game Setup

### Table Setup

*(Image showing game setup with Draw Pile, Discard Pile, Nursery, and Stable areas for multiple players)*

### Your Stable

*(Image showing a player's Stable with Unicorns and Upgrade/Downgrade cards)*

1.  **Separate the Baby Unicorn cards from the main deck.** Each player starts with one Baby Unicorn card in their **Stable**. Place the remaining Baby Unicorn cards in a pile in the center of the playing area. This is the **Nursery**.
2.  **Shuffle the remaining cards** (Unicorns, Magic, Upgrades, Downgrades, Instants) together to form the **Draw Pile**.
3.  **Deal 5 cards to each player.** This is your starting hand. Keep your hand a secret from other players.
4.  **Place the Draw Pile** in the center of the playing area.
5.  **Leave space next to the Draw Pile** for a **Discard Pile**.
6.  **Each player should have a Stable area** in front of them. This is where you play your Unicorns, Upgrades, and Downgrades.

## Gameplay

The game proceeds in turns, starting with the player who last rode a real, imaginary, or toy unicorn. If you can't decide, the youngest player goes first.

### Phases of Your Turn

At the beginning of your turn, you must follow these four phases in order:

1.  Beginning of Turn
2.  Draw Phase
3.  Action Phase
4.  End of Turn

### Beginning of Turn

Some card effects happen at the "beginning of your turn." If you have any such cards in your **Stable**, resolve their effects now.

### Draw Phase

Draw a card from the **Draw Pile**.

### Action Phase

During your Action Phase, you can do **ONE** of the following:

1.  Play a **Unicorn Card**
2.  Play a **Magic Card**
3.  Play an **Upgrade Card**
4.  Play a **Downgrade Card**
5.  Draw a card from the **Draw Pile**.

*You may play an **Instant card** from your hand at any time.*

### End of Turn

If you have more than 7 cards in your hand at the end of your turn, you must **DISCARD** down to 7 cards.

## Card Anatomy

*(Image of a card with labels pointing to different sections)*

1.  **Card Name:** The name of the card.
2.  **Card Type:** Indicates if it's a Unicorn, Magic, Upgrade, Downgrade, or Instant card.
3.  **Unicorn Type (for Unicorn cards):** Baby, Basic, or a special type (e.g., Magical, Naughty, etc.).
4.  **Effects/Description:** Explains what the card does.
5.  **Magic Cost (for Magic cards):** The number of Unicorns you must sacrifice to play the card (if any).
6.  **Flavor Text:** Fun, non-gameplay text.
7.  **Collector Number:** For collection purposes.
8.  **Expansion Symbol:** Indicates which expansion the card belongs to.

## Card Types

### Unicorn Cards

*(Image of a Unicorn card)*

Unicorn cards are the main focus of your **Stable**. Your goal is to collect the required number of Unicorns to win the game.

* **Baby Unicorn Cards:** These cards start in the **Nursery**. Every player starts the game with one Baby Unicorn in their **Stable**.
* **Basic Unicorn Cards:** These are your standard Unicorns with no special effects.
* **Magical Unicorn Cards:** These Unicorns have special effects that can impact the game.

### Magic Cards

*(Image of a Magic card)*

Magic cards are one-time effects that can be played during your Action Phase. After a Magic card's effect is resolved, it is placed in the **Discard Pile**. Some Magic cards require you to **SACRIFICE** a Unicorn. This means moving one of your Unicorn cards from your **Stable** to the **Discard Pile**.

### Upgrade Cards

*(Image of an Upgrade card)*

Upgrade cards have continuous effects that give you an advantage. When you play an Upgrade card, you place it face-up in your **Stable**.

### Downgrade Cards

*(Image of a Downgrade card)*

Downgrade cards have continuous effects that hinder another player. When you play a Downgrade card, you place it face-up in an opponent's **Stable**.

### Instant Cards

*(Image of an Instant card)*

Instant cards can be played at any time, even if it's not your turn. Once an Instant card's effect is resolved, it is placed in the **Discard Pile**.

### Baby Unicorn Cards

*(Image of a Baby Unicorn card)*

Baby Unicorn cards are a special type of Basic Unicorn. Each player starts with one in their **Stable**. The rest are placed in the **Nursery**. Whenever a card effect instructs you to "bring a Baby Unicorn directly into your Stable from the Nursery," you take one card from the **Nursery** pile and add it to your **Stable**.

## Chain Order

The Chain Order applies whenever multiple Instant cards are played in response to a single card or effect.

When an Instant card is played, other players have an opportunity to play their own Instant cards in response. This continues until all players pass. The Instant cards are then resolved in reverse order of how they were played, starting with the last Instant card played.

**Example:**
Player A plays a Magic card. Player B plays an Instant card in response. Player C plays an Instant card in response to Player B's Instant.

The Chain resolves as follows:
1.  Player C's Instant card effect
2.  Player B's Instant card effect
3.  Player A's Magic card effect

Some cards have unique effects that might seem to contradict the general rules. In such cases, the specific card's text takes precedence.

## Optional Rules

### Party Leader

* Before the game starts, each player chooses a Party Leader card and places it face-up in front of them.
* Each Party Leader has a unique effect that is active throughout the game.
* If you have a Party Leader, you collect one fewer Unicorn to win the game.

### Challenge Rules

* **Challenging a Play:** When another player plays a Unicorn, Upgrade, Downgrade, or Magic card, you can play an Instant card to "Challenge" their play.
* **If the Challenge succeeds:** The played card is immediately moved to the Discard Pile and its effect does not resolve.
* **If the Challenge fails:** The played card's effect resolves as normal.
* **Challenging an Instant:** You cannot Challenge the playing of an Instant card.

## Frequently Asked Questions

* **Can I play a Magic card during another player's turn?**
    No, unless a card explicitly states otherwise. Magic cards are played during your Action Phase.

* **What if a card tells me to SACRIFICE a Unicorn, but I have no Unicorns in my Stable?**
    You cannot play that card.

* **Can I choose not to activate a card's effect if I don't want to?**
    No, if a card has a "when this card enters your Stable" or "at the beginning of your turn" effect, you must resolve it. If a card has an "if you choose to" or "you may" clause, then you have the option.

* **What happens if I run out of cards in the Draw Pile?**
    Shuffle the Discard Pile to create a new Draw Pile.
